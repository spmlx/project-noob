About NMIXX

NMIXX is known for its MIXX POP concept, blending two or more genres within a single song to create unexpected transitions and a distinctive sound. Their music combines pop, hip-hop, R&B, rock, and electronic elements, showcasing their versatility, powerful vocals, and experimental approach to K-pop.
