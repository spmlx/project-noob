About EDEN

EDEN, whose real name is Jonathon Ng, is an Irish singer, songwriter, and music producer known for his atmospheric sound, emotional lyrics, and unique blend of indie pop, electronic music, and alternative rock. His music explores themes of love, loneliness, heartbreak, and personal growth, creating a dreamy and melancholic atmosphere that resonates deeply with listeners.
